Push Burst Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Push Burst (the "App"), made by Project Unknown ("we", "us"), uses and why. We do not run our own servers for the App and we do not ask you to create an account.

1. Information stored on your device

The App saves your game progress and settings on your device only: best score, cleared puzzle stages, theme and sound/vibration settings, tutorial status, remaining free hints, and whether you bought No Ads. We do not receive this information. Deleting the App deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device such as:

  • the device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android),
  • IP address, device model, operating system and language,
  • information about the ads shown and your interaction with them.

On iOS the App asks for your permission before the advertising identifier can be used for tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized using that identifier. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent using Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings > PRIVACY in the App.

You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Leaderboards and achievements

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), the App sends your Classic mode scores and unlocked achievements to that service so they appear on leaderboards. Your player name and profile are managed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your account details.

4. Purchases

The No Ads purchase is processed by the App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your payment information. The store tells the App only whether the purchase was made, so ads can be removed and purchases can be restored.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers named above (Google AdMob, Apple Game Center, Google Play Games, the App Store and Google Play) for the purposes described in this policy.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
